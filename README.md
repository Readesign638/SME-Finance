# 🇱🇸 Khotso-Pay · SME Finance

**A business app for Lesotho SMEs — sales, inventory, debts, reports, and AI.**

## The Problem

Small businesses in Lesotho track sales in notebooks, manage stock from memory, and forget who owes them. Excel is too complex. QuickBooks is too expensive.

Khotso-Pay is built for this market: LSL currency, M-Pesa and EcoCash, WhatsApp reminders, English and Sesotho.

## Features

- **Sales** — record paid sales by method
- **Debts (Sekoloto)** — track who owes what, send WhatsApp/SMS/Email reminders
- **Inventory** — products, stock, low-stock alerts
- **Reports** — PDF cash-up, Excel/CSV export
- **AI Assistant** — ask about your real business data
- **Business Modes** — app adapts to Products, Services, or Debts-only

## Tech

React · TypeScript · Tailwind · Vite · Firebase · Gemini AI · PWA

## Limitations

- Single user per business
- Records payments, doesn't process them through APIs
